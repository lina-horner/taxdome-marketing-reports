What the data says

The gap to plan is widest at the top and closes as you move down. Conversion is running ahead of plan even though lead volume sits below it, so the constraint is intake, not the funnel itself.

SQL is the strongest signal. At 119 percent of Plan V3 and accelerating on both a monthly and annual basis, it points to customer output above target once the normal close lag clears.

ACV recovered 24 percent month over month off the tax season low but remains below the prior baseline. Volume is ahead of plan, value is behind it, so deal value and mix are the lever to manage next.
