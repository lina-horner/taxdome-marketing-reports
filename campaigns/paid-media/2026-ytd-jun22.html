The call

Paid works at the top of the funnel. Year to date it has brought 718 MQLs and 220 demos, and the demos that reach a decision close at about 22%. That part is healthy.

What paid is worth in revenue depends on the model: $28K if we count only deals it sourced, up to $540K if we count every customer it influenced. The honest answer is somewhere in that range. The $28K is also still climbing, because $170K of the paid deals created this year have not closed yet.

Recommendation: keep funding paid at $156K. Judge it next quarter on two things, whether that open pipeline closes near 22% and whether more of paid's demos are ICP firms. Run a holdout test to learn how many of these customers we would have won anyway.
