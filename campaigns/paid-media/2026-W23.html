What stands out

Efficiency winner: Delivery

$53.26 per lead at a 2.71% CTR. The strongest cost per lead in the account this week and the clearest candidate to scale.

Needs attention: CCH Axcess

956 clicks but only 9 leads, a $115.38 CPL. High click volume with weak conversion points to a landing page or offer leak, not a targeting problem.

Google is conquesting competitors

The lone Google campaign targets competitor search terms and pulls a 4.60% CTR at a CPL in line with Meta. Room to broaden once tracking is solid.

LinkedIn is brand, and paused

14 clicks on 3,099 impressions is normal for a video brand play, not a lead motion. It is currently paused, so it is not feeding pipeline right now.

Window and scope. June 1 to 7, 2026, month to date. US and Canada only. International markets are excluded.

Result definitions differ by channel. Meta results are platform pixel leads. Google results are conversions. LinkedIn is a brand awareness objective with no lead goal, so it is excluded from results and CPL.

Blended CPL is calculated across the lead-gen channels only, Meta and Google. It excludes LinkedIn spend.

Source. Figures are platform reported. HubSpot currently captures about 63% of paid contacts, with an attribution fix in progress, so platform totals run ahead of what is credited in the CRM.
